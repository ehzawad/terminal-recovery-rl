## Review the S, R and D training pipeline and the next registered steps (training-plan-reviewer)

**Result: do not start S, R, or D yet.** The mask and Dr.GRPO normalization paths are largely consistent with the installed TRL 1.14 implementation, and the S and R LoRA target patterns match. The material problems are the mismatch between the variance gate’s groups and R’s actual groups, unproven memory and adapter synchronization at useful trajectory lengths, unenforced S and D budgets, and a final analysis that can declare a pass on incomplete or incorrectly selected data. This was a read-only review; I did not run training or use the GPU.

### Verified findings and recommendations

1. **The variance gate does not measure R’s within-group variance.** [gates.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/gates.py:75) groups four *different trials* by task. [make_rows.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/make_rows.py:65) independently assigns each trial clean or faulted. TRL’s [RepeatSampler](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/.venvs/rl/lib/python3.12/site-packages/trl/trainer/utils.py:890) instead repeats one dataset row four times, preserving its fault assignment; TRL centers reward within those four completions at [grpo_trainer.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/.venvs/rl/lib/python3.12/site-packages/trl/trainer/grpo_trainer.py:2819). The current gate can count variation caused by changing the *environment* between trials even when the actual RL group has no learning signal. **Recommendation:** before training, measure four independent P completions for each identical `(task, fault family, fault seed)` row and apply the registered thresholds to those groups. Record this as a dated gate amendment; do not present the existing gate as a validation of R’s gradient supply.

2. **R’s sampling mix and task overlap with S are not enforced.** [train_grpo.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_grpo.py:94) accepts any rows file; [train_grpo.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_grpo.py:145) gives 16 trajectories per update, but TRL shuffles and repeats rows by its sampler at [grpo_trainer.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/.venvs/rl/lib/python3.12/site-packages/trl/trainer/grpo_trainer.py:1260). S independently takes the first 512 rows of each trial slice at [run_arm_s.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/run_arm_s.py:44). Neither path proves an achieved 75/25 trajectory mix or the registered “same training tasks” comparison in [PREREGISTRATION.md](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/PREREGISTRATION.md:52) and [A2](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/PREREGISTRATION.md:147). **Recommendation:** freeze row manifests and audit actual attempts by task and fault status for both arms before fitting; make the 1,024-attempt cap and mix checks executable.

3. **S’s 18 GPU-hour cap and D’s two-hour fit are not implemented as arm-level limits.** S’s two collection calls and two fits at [run_arm_s.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/run_arm_s.py:65) have no shared timer. Each [train_sft.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_sft.py:61) invocation defaults to three hours, with its timer starting after model loading at [train_sft.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_sft.py:83). D has no driver that filters R’s verified safe successes, invokes the same recipe with a two-hour limit, and records its candidates, although [PREREGISTRATION.md](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/PREREGISTRATION.md:55) requires it. **Recommendation:** add one audited timer and attempt ledger around all of S, plus an explicit D collection/filter/fit driver with its registered limit and candidate checkpoints.

4. **Checkpoint selection is specified but not performed or validated.** R saves at steps 32 and 64 at [train_grpo.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_grpo.py:173); S lists four candidates at [run_arm_s.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/run_arm_s.py:77). [run_eval_arms.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/run_eval_arms.py:22) evaluates whatever adapters its caller names, with no dev_monitor selector or proof the chosen path was frozen before test. S also unconditionally uses round-one epoch 2 for round-two collection at [run_arm_s.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/run_arm_s.py:71); the registration does not clearly say whether that intermediate choice is fixed or selected. **Recommendation:** codify the dev_monitor metric, ties, candidate availability after an early stop, D’s equivalent opportunity, and the round-two actor rule; save a signed selection manifest before any test run.

5. **The 6K-token systems gate is unmet.** R’s configured maximum is 6,144 completion tokens at [train_grpo.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_grpo.py:136). The existing [smoke summary](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/runs/grpo_smoke_v3/run_summary.json:1) reports 43.09 GiB torch peak with maximum *generated* completion length 1,579 in its first logged update, and zero loss/gradient with all rewards equal in that update ([same file](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/runs/grpo_smoke_v3/run_summary.json:7)). The later vLLM share change to 0.40 in [train_grpo.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_grpo.py:135) has not been measured in this evidence. S’s [logits_to_keep path](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_sft.py:95) still holds selected logits and their loss graph for a long sequence. **Recommendation:** run bounded, representative 6K completion/8K total forward-backward probes for R *and* S/D, including a nonzero-reward R update, and record device peak, torch peak, finite gradients, masked-token counts, and wall time against the 44 GiB gate. The short, zero-gradient smoke cannot clear it.

6. **A nonzero adapter synchronization has not been verified.** TRL does call `sync_weights()` when the optimizer step changes at [grpo_trainer.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/.venvs/rl/lib/python3.12/site-packages/trl/trainer/grpo_trainer.py:1853), and its colocated implementation merges PEFT weights before loading vLLM at [vllm_generation.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/.venvs/rl/lib/python3.12/site-packages/trl/generation/vllm_generation.py:443). That establishes an intended path, not that this Qwen adapter affects the next rollout: the observed smoke update had zero gradient. **Recommendation:** make one known nonzero update, compare adapter tensor hashes and fixed-prompt token log probabilities before and after on the training model and colocated vLLM, then verify the exported adapter through the evaluation server. This is required by [gate 1](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/PREREGISTRATION.md:74).

7. **R’s liveness callback omits a registered rule and watches the wrong kind of clipping for another.** [train_grpo.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_grpo.py:75) checks zero-variance streaks and `completions/clipped_ratio`, but has no two-window `<60% informative groups` stop from [gate 4](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/PREREGISTRATION.md:82). In TRL, `completions/clipped_ratio` means completions lacking EOS at [grpo_trainer.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/.venvs/rl/lib/python3.12/site-packages/trl/trainer/grpo_trainer.py:2351); policy probability clipping is separately logged as `clip_ratio/region_mean` at [grpo_trainer.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/.venvs/rl/lib/python3.12/site-packages/trl/trainer/grpo_trainer.py:3331). **Recommendation:** implement the 128-group informative windows from actual generated groups and state before training which clipping measure the registered 5% rule means; monitor both diagnostics.

8. **The D rollout record needs an audited extraction contract.** [train_grpo.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_grpo.py:42) logs prompt IDs, completion IDs, tool mask and verdict after scoring, which is the right raw material for D. But it has no unique attempt ID, logged semantic reward, explicit truncation/loss mask, or resume deduplication; [train_sft.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_sft.py:31) silently filters records and skips overlength successes. TRL zeroes the entire completion loss mask for truncated trajectories at [grpo_trainer.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/.venvs/rl/lib/python3.12/site-packages/trl/trainer/grpo_trainer.py:2537). **Recommendation:** validate one-to-one coverage of R attempts, masks and IDs, specify whether D includes successful R rollouts whose R loss was masked, and report every filtered or skipped success before D fitting. Do not silently reduce “every verified success” from [PREREGISTRATION.md](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/PREREGISTRATION.md:55).

9. **There is a boundary mismatch between TRL and the shared evaluation/S collection loop.** TRL stops its tool loop after 16 iterations at [grpo_trainer.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/.venvs/rl/lib/python3.12/site-packages/trl/trainer/grpo_trainer.py:2025). The evaluation loop at [rollout.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/termrl/rollout.py:139) can sample one further assistant turn after the 16th tool result and only then stop if that turn requests another tool. This can change both the final answer and the token record at the cap, contrary to the byte-identical-loop claim in [A1](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/PREREGISTRATION.md:99). **Recommendation:** align the end condition and compare token IDs, tool masks and terminal results on fixtures that reach exactly 16 calls and both token ceilings.

10. **The final comparison can pass on incomplete or stale evaluation files.** [analyze.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/analyze.py:31) silently overwrites duplicate `(task, trial)` keys; [analyze.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/analyze.py:73) intersects arms and merely reports missing episodes; [analyze.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/analyze.py:80) can then declare the pilot rule passed. [evaluate.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/evaluate.py:88) resumes by task/trial alone, without checking row, prompt, model checkpoint or decoding identity. **Recommendation:** fail closed unless P, S, D and R have exactly one result for every frozen test row with matching row/config/checkpoint hashes and no missing episodes; make the blind audit a separate required sign-off before a final “passed” label.

11. **The analysis defaults to the wrong headline and makes recovery differences from different observed subsets.** The frozen headline is recovery, while [analyze.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/analyze.py:62) defaults to collateral. Its recovery metric includes a trial only when *that arm* observed the fault at [analyze.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/analyze.py:26), and [analyze.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/analyze.py:44) intersects tasks after that arm-specific filtering. Thus its displayed difference can compare different trials within the same task, and changing observation frequency can change the estimate. **Recommendation:** require the frozen headline as an input and report fault observation rates and denominators for each arm; before training, register a paired mechanism estimand and handling of trials observed in only one arm. Keep the originally registered conditional incidence visible as well.

12. **Registered secondary reporting is incomplete.** [analyze.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/analyze.py:20) reports neither pass⁴ nor tokens, turns and time, despite [PREREGISTRATION.md](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/PREREGISTRATION.md:69), and it does not require the clean retention and held-out-family files. **Recommendation:** add a completeness check and a predeclared report over the separate faulted, clean and held-out test manifests, with the registered secondary measures and uncertainty intervals.

### Checks that appear correct

The SFT target alignment at [train_sft.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_sft.py:90) supervises only model tokens, including the model token immediately after a tool result; dividing each accumulated token-loss sum by the group’s supervised-token count is internally consistent. TRL multiplies its completion and tool masks at [grpo_trainer.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/.venvs/rl/lib/python3.12/site-packages/trl/trainer/grpo_trainer.py:3082), centers without standard-deviation scaling at [grpo_trainer.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/.venvs/rl/lib/python3.12/site-packages/trl/trainer/grpo_trainer.py:2819), and divides Dr.GRPO by the fixed maximum completion length and accumulation count at [grpo_trainer.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/.venvs/rl/lib/python3.12/site-packages/trl/trainer/grpo_trainer.py:3222). S and R use the same LoRA target regex at [train_sft.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_sft.py:27) and [train_grpo.py](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/scripts/train_grpo.py:31); the saved smoke adapter contains 400 tensors across the intended text projections. These checks do **not** establish that an updated adapter reaches either vLLM server.

### Questions 2 and 3

**Before training:** fix the gate/group mismatch and analysis fail-closed behavior; freeze and audit the S/R row manifests, budgets and checkpoint procedure; establish D’s exact extraction and fit; align the 16-turn loop; and pass a representative 6K memory probe plus a nonzero-update adapter-sync probe. Those are dependencies for a comparable pilot, not optional polish.

**On the 75/25 mix:** it remains the registered design at [A2](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/PREREGISTRATION.md:147) and [A4](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/PREREGISTRATION.md:163). Now that recovery is the headline and clean collateral was 0/106 in the shared gate result, allocating three quarters of training to clean episodes has a clear risk of spending budget on low-signal groups. That is an inference, not a measured failure of the mix. Preserve 75/25 for the current registered gate and stop rule; any switch to fault-heavy training needs a dated amendment before training, with S and R changed together and clean retention kept as a required outcome.

**If the variance gate fails:** report that the registered 75/25, group-four RL experiment stopped at gate 3. Do not rescue it by silently changing group size, task difficulty, fault share, or start checkpoint. The strongest honest next registration is a *new pilot* with faulted-only or pre-specified fault-heavy training, identical S/R task and attempt budgets, four completions under the **same fixed fault per group**, a fresh prospective variance gate on that actual group distribution, and a pre-specified harder-task rule based only on train-side evidence. Keep P, S and D competitive and the still-sealed test untouched until all configurations and dev_monitor selections are frozen. If test outcomes have already been opened, the revised study needs a genuinely new held-out set for a prospective claim. It would still be a single-seed pilot under the independence limit in [PREREGISTRATION.md](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/PREREGISTRATION.md:89).

**Open decisions for the owner:** whether the next registration should prioritize faulted-only training or a specified fault-heavy mix; whether S’s round-two actor is fixed at epoch 2 or chosen on dev_monitor; and whether the 5% “clipping” gate means length truncation, policy-ratio clipping, or both. None should be resolved after seeing R or test outcomes.
