There is material open data beyond Endless Terminals. The best next acquisition is an audited, offline subset of SETA, followed by TMax repair tasks and LiteCoder-Terminal-RL. Stack Overflow and Unix & Linux Stack Exchange can supply the seeds for executable RL environments; they do not supply a reliable reward merely by containing accepted answers. InterCode-Bash is already executable, despite its placement among non-executable sources in the brief. Several newer papers describe useful environments without a verifiable public, licensed dataset release; those are methods to consider, not inventory to promise.

**Endless Terminals.** The paper appeared January 23, 2026 and reports 3,255 Apptainer tasks, approximately 2,500 converted to Harbor; the pinned public Harbor release contains 2,492. Use the count for the artifact being acquired. It supplies instructions, environment construction, tests, and solutions. Its synthetic taxonomy overlaps the skills in other terminal collections; this is not evidence of copied tasks. The authors say generation preceded TB2.0's release; that is a useful provenance claim, not independent semantic clearance. ET directly overlaps this project's own train/dev/test pool, so no ET variant is a new external test. Offline operation requires prebuilt dependencies and replacement of downloading verifier wrappers, work already done for the selected local slice. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.16443v1), [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/obiwan96/endless-terminals).

The local evidence is stronger than the word “verified” on a card: 609 candidates became 593 valid in the earlier slice, but the hard-tail admission retained only 84 tasks/83 groups. Twenty graders failed mutation discrimination; 68 tasks were excluded by instruction/test review, with overlapping reasons. Fifty-five of those excluded tasks had passed both automated checks. These findings show why reference-pass plus no-op-fail is insufficient. The hard tail cannot simply be treated as extra useful difficulty. [Registration and completed admission record](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/PREREGISTRATION.md:449), [prompt/headroom context](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/data/audit/council_r7/context.md:24).

**SETA.** The July 12, 2026 paper and downloadable card establish real Q&A-to-environment conversion. Across Synth and Evolve, the inventory contains 1,140 Stack Overflow, 562 Unix/Linux SE, 1,796 Ask Ubuntu, 285 NL2Bash, and 784 Kaggle records. My recount of the earlier manifest gives 3,465 groups after stripping evolution suffixes and collapsing the identified question subvariants; that is an operational grouping, not a proof of independent problems. Harbor folders contain Dockerfiles, `solve.sh`, and tests. NL2Bash-derived SETA rows overlap the NL2Bash/InterCode/OpenThoughts source family. Stack Exchange posts and evolved descendants must remain together across splits. No broad ET/TB semantic-contamination clearance was established here. [Card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/camel-ai/SETA-Env), [pipeline code](https://github.com/camel-ai/seta).

SETA is a promising Linux operations source, but the existing local service audit inspected 34 tasks/30 roots and found installer/fetch commands in 33 verifier scripts. Bases included Ubuntu 24.04/22.04, Debian, and Rocky. Prestage packages and verifier dependencies, supervise services without assuming a full systemd boot, and distinguish loopback services from internet requirements. Discard or separately redesign cloud, external-network, privileged-kernel, and nested-Docker cases. A card declaring Apache-2.0 does not resolve rights in copied Stack Exchange text or third-party notebooks; retain source attribution and the applicable upstream licence instead of treating the whole derivation as provenance-free Apache. The owner's acceptance of CC BY-SA makes that practical. [Local audit](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/data/audit/AUDIT_REPORT.md:63).

**TMax-15K.** This is the largest clearly licensed executable candidate checked here, with a June 22, 2026 paper. Its canonical release is a 60,330,513-byte parquet plus a 452,939,754-byte task archive. Fields include initial/final tests and Apptainer-style `container_def`; do not mistake the metadata for fully portable Docker build contexts. The card links an Open-Instruct training conversion and declares ODC-BY, while noting Gemini-output terms and responsible-use guidance. Preserve these notices. TMax expands the taxonomy-style synthesis used by ET; shared approach does not establish task identity. [Canonical card and training-conversion link](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/TMax-15K).

The prior local audit found 213 nominal short/moderate repair candidates, not 213 admitted environments. Six of seven deliberately suspicious inspected tasks exposed expected-answer material, not a random-sample prevalence estimate. One construction runs a correct solver into `/tmp/expected_values.txt` and removes only the solver. Quarantine such tasks until answer artifacts are removed from the agent image and graders derive truth independently. Convert definitions, restore archive fixtures referenced through author-local paths, pin compiler/service dependencies, and audit complete image contents. TMax's paper reports Qwen3.5-9B TB2.1 improving from 16.1 to 28.8 with its RL data, versus 25.5 using ET. Its tested SFT mixtures did not consistently improve that base. These results use much greater compute and a different horizon/harness than this LoRA pilot; checkpoints were selected on TBLite. Its 13-token audit reports 0% overlap with TB2.0/TBLite for TMax, ET, CLI-Gym, TermiGen, and OpenThoughts; TerminalTraj has 0.5%/0.2% respectively. **LiteCoder.** The repository dates its release April 13, 2026; the paper appeared May 28. The SFT trajectories and 602 RL environments are separate resources. SFT has an average 27.4 turns according to the paper, so it is materially more relevant to multi-step behavior than NL-to-one-command pairs, but not automatically compatible with a 16-command budget. The paper describes 13-gram filtering against Terminal-Bench; I additionally found zero identical normalized RL instructions against the local 2,492 ET, 89 TB2.1, and 100 TBLite tasks. This does not exclude paraphrases or shared solutions. [Repository](https://github.com/icip-cas/LiteCoder), [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29559v1), [SFT card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Lite-Coder/LiteCoder-Terminal-SFT).

All 602 RL metadata rows request internet access, all request zero GPUs, and 601 specify at most 8 GiB RAM; agent timeouts range from 1,800 to 5,400 seconds. These are declarations, not measurements. Every Dockerfile in the eight-task systematic sample uses Ubuntu 24.04, and every sampled verifier installs packages using apt/curl/uv. Several task instructions concern local services or Git and may be portable offline; installation tasks need cached packages or a declared change to task semantics. Prebuilding the thing the agent is supposed to install would invalidate the task. One sample also both says a corruption-setup script is provided and tells the agent to write it: inspect semantics before treating repair labels as natural faults. [RL card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Lite-Coder/LiteCoder-Terminal-RL-preview), [measured sample](measured_inventory.json), [sample instruction](sources/lite_sample/fix-corrupt-git-repo/instruction.md).

It is therefore unusually close to the recovery goal, although synthetic corruption can be contrived and much work is Python/runtime repair rather than shell administration. The public card's old 1,321-instance paragraph is superseded by its release note and 1,655-row inventory. It is not an ET-derived task set; split by repository and corruption family. TB lexical-overlap evidence is the limited TMax audit above. Prefetch the SWE-smith base images and all `COPY` fixtures, convert the older task/Compose format, and separately check missing fixture delivery. A four-megabyte parquet is not the size of the runnable environments. Protect the grading toolchain because repairing a Python runtime necessarily lets the agent modify code a naïve verifier might trust. [Card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-thoughts/OpenThoughts-Agent-v1-RL), [code](https://github.com/open-thoughts/OpenThoughts-Agent).

Its broader tooling includes compilation, services, distributed computing, and GPU-related work, so a local/offline CPU-only subset and complete reference-solution check are necessary. It is a synthesis relative of ET, not demonstrated to duplicate ET. Group near-identical easy/medium/hard variants. **InterCode-Bash.** The June 2023 InterCode release is MIT and does provide execution rewards. Its current reward deserves particular caution. Source inspection shows roughly equal components for Git-status changes, file content, and TF-IDF output similarity. The content filter includes `A`, `??`, and `C`, but omits `M`; output similarity is not an exact semantic assertion. The agent can also interact with its Git metadata. These are static weaknesses, not a newly executed exploit. Replace or validate them with task-specific immutable-state checks before optimizing GRPO against this reward. There are three verified precedents for converting command/Q&A knowledge into executable tasks, with different strength of connection to Stack Exchange. SETA directly turns SO/Unix/Ask Ubuntu sources into task specifications, Dockerfiles, solutions, and tests. It filters source quality using acceptance/votes, checks no-op and oracle behavior, and audits all-failure rollout cases with a separate judge; the paper reports finding 94 design flaws this way. Its authors explicitly acknowledge that jointly generated solutions and tests can share unstated assumptions. InterCode operationalizes the NL2Bash subset with concrete filesystems and differential execution. OpenThoughts releases NL2Bash-verified tasks after verifier/build stability filtering. Only the first is direct evidence for a broad Stack Exchange-to-terminal-RL pipeline; the other two establish the narrower command-pair conversion. [SETA methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.10891v1), [InterCode paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14898), [OpenThoughts card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-thoughts/OpenThoughts-Agent-v1-RL).

My proposed conversion procedure is to start with reproducible, observable questions, not dump-wide imitation. Retain question, answers, comments, revisions and provenance; treat the accepted answer as a candidate reference, not ground truth. Extract explicit requirements, package/OS versions, initial files, identities and permissions, observable failure, allowed changes, and desired final state. Reject posts depending on unavailable hardware, private logs, production credentials, real cloud accounts, irretrievable downloads, or irreducibly ambiguous goals. Close missing details explicitly in a derived instruction rather than silently adding them only to tests. Split source questions, duplicate clusters and related bug families before generating variants.

Construct a deterministic initial environment with real failing commands and locally seeded fixtures. Install dependencies during image preparation; if installing software is the actual task, provide a local package/wheel/repository cache and leave the target package uninstalled. Pin images and packages, fix locale/time where relevant, and provide required local documentation. No-op must fail substantive completion checks. Independently implement and replay the accepted solution and at least one alternative where feasible, twice from fresh images. A solution that works on an old answer author's machine but fails on the pinned image is evidence to repair the specification or reject the task, not evidence that the learning agent is wrong.

Write checkers from the stated contract. Check semantic file content, permissions/ownership, service behavior, preserved inputs, and relevant regressions; do not require the accepted answer's exact command string. Grade using original immutable fixtures or independently computed expected results. Keep reference answers and tests out of the agent image, including temporary files, setup scripts, layer history accessible to the agent, logs, Git history and environment variables. Use external or otherwise protected checks rather than an interpreter, utility, reward file or test runner the agent can replace. Kill or contain background agent processes before sensitive grading without destroying the legitimate service state being tested.

A concrete Unix.SE-derived candidate would start a loopback Nginx service under an unprivileged account with a misconfigured include or unreadable static directory. The instruction would require restoring a working `/health` response and a named log format while preserving unrelated sites and secrets. Checks would validate configuration, send fresh nonce-bearing requests, inspect log semantics, compare protected files, and recheck behavior after a supervised restart. Wrong mutations would include returning the right text from the wrong endpoint, disabling access controls globally, printing a fake success message, deleting other site configs, hard-coding one request, or writing the reward directly. A shell-text question could instead seed randomized filenames with spaces/newlines/symlinks and test exact selection/preservation across several fixtures. These examples are proposed new constructions, not claims that a particular downloaded task already implements them.

Main conversion failures are incomplete environments; version or GNU/BSD differences; an answer that solves a narrower problem; popularity mistaken for correctness; tests and reference sharing the same error; tests enforcing unstated conventions; answer leakage; permissive presence-only grading; nondeterministic timing/process behavior; network dependencies hidden in setup or grading; and reward hacking through mutable tooling. Randomized fixtures, independent checker review, alternative valid solutions, negative/mutation tests, clean resets, and multi-run offline replay address different failure classes. None replaces the others. If success depends on a privileged kernel operation, an actual reboot, or a remote service, a normal Docker `--network none` environment is not a faithful setting; exclude it or explicitly define a different task. Loopback HTTP inside one container remains available with external networking disabled.

The present harness is a useful starting point but does not make every Harbor task safe for RL. The ET path snapshots the agent filesystem and mounts a trusted Python/pytest toolchain; the TB path intentionally executes native tests in the same task container and reads its reward file. The latter preserves benchmark scoring but needs additional protection before optimizing a policy against arbitrary imported tasks. File-artifact tasks can often use a clean trusted grader; service/runtime-repair tasks need protected behavioral checks that preserve or recreate the target service state. [ET verifier](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/termrl/verify.py:1), [native grading path](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/termrl/tb.py:205), [offline resource-limited sandbox](/mnt/sdb/arafat/ehz/llm/terminal-recovery-rl/termrl/sandbox.py:95).

For this box, “good training data” means valid specifications, discriminating and protected rewards, reproducible offline resets, source-independent held-out tasks, and useful failure modes under the actual prompted Qwen3.5-9B. First audit a small stratified queue, for example 24 SETA, 16 TMax and 8 LiteCoder tasks. That is a proposed audit allocation, not a statistical guarantee. After model-free admission, probe several attempts per training root using the deployed prompt and budget; favor training tasks with both genuine successes and failures, and distinguish verifier/build failures from policy failures. All-fail tasks may belong in a teacher/SFT curriculum or be invalid; all-pass tasks offer little outcome-RL signal. Do not select the sealed test by observed Qwen difficulty.

No new dataset's runtime or long-trajectory training footprint was measured in this survey. The measured ET trajectory rate and successful 32K serving probe cannot be extrapolated to longer GRPO updates, compilers, databases, or many heavy images. An implementation pass still needs build/reset/grading wall time, CPU/RAM/disk usage, offline reference replay, and a representative nonzero-gradient update. Count agent and grader containers together against the existing concurrency cap. Useful efficiency measures are admitted independent tasks per audit hour and informative rollout groups per GPU-hour, not downloaded rows. Keep a prompted-base and matched SFT/rejection-sampling comparison so that command-format imitation is not mistaken for an RL benefit.

Open decisions for the owner are the acceptable CPU/image-build and data-curation budget; whether task horizons may exceed 16 commands; whether local services and runtime-repair tasks are in scope when they require stronger grading isolation; and whether to obtain licence clarification for TermiGen/OpenThoughts/TerminalTraj. These do not block a SETA/TMax/LiteCoder admission queue. No decision to ingest new data, rebuild training, or publish a derived Stack Exchange corpus is claimed by this survey.

Ranked recommendation: **1. SETA's local Linux operations and troubleshooting subset**, with source-question grouping, upstream attribution, independent checker review, and offline packaging. It directly expands beyond ET's file-transform emphasis and already performs the expensive Q&A-to-environment step. **2. TMax's repair/debugging/build subset**, starting from the existing 213-candidate audit queue; it has the strongest checked exact-model RL evidence, but answer leakage, fixture restoration and execution cost require screening. **3. LiteCoder's 602 RL environments**, prioritizing CPU-only local service/Git/data tasks; use a small, verified slice of its SFT trajectories only where the base lacks useful successful behavior. **4. CLI-Gym**, if runtime/dependency recovery is the target and the image/fixture/grader work is affordable. **5. NL2Bash/NLC2CMD, tldr and matching man pages** as targeted SFT or offline references, not the main multi-step RL curriculum.
